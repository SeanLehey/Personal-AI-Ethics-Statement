# Personal AI Ethics Statement

#### Where I <ins>Don't</ins> Use AI Tools:
I don't use LLMs to do my writing for me. I understand that LLMs can be a useful tool for helping with writing and editing, but I prefer to communicate without their assistance. As a former editor, I've developed my own voice and therefore don't see the need to offload my writing to an LLM.

#### Where I <ins>Do</ins> Use AI Tools:
I use LLMs to help me learn how to code. It's my goal to understand what the LLM is teaching me, and I make sure to avoid blindly copying and pasting LLM-generated code output into my projects. An LLM should teach by example, but I still have an obligation to comprehend what it's doing and why.

I plan and outline scripts, interpret what is produced, and adjust them accordingly, but the initial rough generation is a task I've become comfortable assigning to LLMs to enhance my workflow. It seems that as LLMs become more robust at coding tasks, our role as humans is shifting more toward the QA side of things, though it still behooves us to understand what we're putting out there.
